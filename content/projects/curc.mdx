## Overview

CurC pairs a medical-grade wearable with a conversational copilot that adapts to each patient's health goals. I designed the experience, architected the stack, and led a hybrid web + mobile release across three countries.

## Highlights

<Callout type="success" title="Signals">
Prototype-to-production in 10 weeks with real-time vitals, personalized nudges, and clinical escalation flows.
</Callout>

- Architected a Flutter app with offline-first caches, realtime Firestore sync, and deep link automations.
- Built Gemini AI prompts that translate sensor streams into natural-language coaching and doctor-ready reports.
- Orchestrated HIPAA compliance with encrypted storage, audit logging, and zero-trust role policies.
